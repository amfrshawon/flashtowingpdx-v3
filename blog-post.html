What to Do Immediately After a Car Breakdown on I-84 (Portland Driver's Emergency Guide)

F
Flash Towing PDX Team
March 15, 2026

It happens in seconds. One moment you're cruising westbound on I-84 through the Columbia Gorge, the next your dashboard lights up, the car stutters, and you're pulling over onto the narrow shoulder with traffic flying past at 70 mph.

In those first moments, most drivers freeze. They don't know what to do, they make panicked decisions, and sometimes they get hurt. After towing thousands of vehicles off Portland's freeways, our dispatch team has seen every scenario. Here is exactly what you should do — in order.

🚨 Emergency? Call First. If your vehicle is in traffic, smoking, or you feel unsafe — call 911 immediately. For roadside assistance and towing, call Flash Towing PDX at (971) 326-8952. We're available 24/7.

Step 1: Get to Safety — First Priority

1

Signal, steer right, and coast to a stop

The moment you sense trouble, activate your hazard lights. Steer as far right as possible — ideally past the white fog line and onto the shoulder. If you can coast further to an exit ramp, do it. Distance from live traffic is your most important priority.

2

Exit the vehicle — on the passenger side

Once stopped, never stand between your car and traffic. Exit from the passenger side or the back of the vehicle. Move up the bank or behind the barrier if one exists. Stand as far from the road as you safely can.

3

Set up warning triangles or flares

If you have warning triangles in your emergency kit, place them 100 feet behind your vehicle. Flares are even better at night. These 60 seconds of setup can save your life — drivers in lane 1 have less than 2 seconds to react at highway speeds.

✅ Pro Tip: Keep an Emergency Kit Every Portland driver should keep: warning triangles, a reflective vest, a flashlight, a phone charger, jumper cables, and a gallon of water in their trunk. $40 kit, potentially life-saving.

Step 2: Assess the Situation

Once you're safely off the road, take a breath and assess:

  • Is anyone injured? If yes, call 911 before anything else.
  • Is the car smoking or leaking fluid? If yes, move further away — at least 100 feet. Do not try to open the hood.
  • Are you in a safe location? A narrow shoulder on a curve is not safe. A wide shoulder on a straight section is much better.
  • What time is it? Night breakdowns are significantly more dangerous — our drivers prioritize these calls accordingly.

Step 3: Call for Help

Now that you're safe, it's time to make calls. Here's the order:

  1. 911 — If there are injuries, fire, or you feel unsafe in your location
  2. Your towing company — (971) 326-8952 for Flash Towing PDX. Tell us your exact location, car make/model, and what happened.
  3. Your insurance company — Most policies include roadside assistance. Call them after the tow truck is dispatched, not before.

How to Tell Us Your Exact Location on I-84

When you call, the most helpful thing you can do is give us a precise location. Look for:

  • Mile markers — Small green signs on the right shoulder with a number
  • Exit numbers — What was the last exit you passed?
  • Cross streets or landmarks — "Past the 33rd Ave overpass" is very helpful
  • Your GPS coordinates — Drop a pin in Google Maps and read us the coordinates

Step 4: While You Wait

4

Stay visible but stay back

Keep your hazards on. Stay outside the vehicle if it's safe — being inside a stopped car on a shoulder has ended badly for many people. But stay behind the barrier or on the bank. Never stand in the road or wave at traffic.

If it's cold or raining heavily and you must stay in the car, stay in the passenger seat (not the driver seat, which is closest to traffic). Keep your seatbelt on.

Common Mistakes Portland Drivers Make

After thousands of freeway tows, here's what we see constantly — and what you should never do:

  • ❌ Getting out of the car before moving to the shoulder — Stay in and steer, don't panic-exit in a live lane
  • ❌ Standing at the back of the car — A driver distracted by their phone can end this situation permanently
  • ❌ Trying to change a tire on a highway shoulder — Get to an exit or parking lot if at all possible
  • ❌ Calling friends before calling a tow — The clock is ticking while you're stopped on a busy shoulder
  • ❌ Leaving the vehicle and walking along the freeway — This is extraordinarily dangerous. Stay near your vehicle.

When We Arrive

Our driver will pull up behind your vehicle with lights flashing. You should:

  • Stay in your safe position until they approach you — don't walk toward the tow truck
  • Show them your registration and tell them where you want the car taken
  • Gather all valuables from the car before it's loaded — once it's on the flatbed, access is limited

We'll take care of everything from there. Our drivers are experts at freeway loading and will have you out of there quickly and safely.
